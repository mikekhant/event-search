# Interest Profile and Filter Rules

Read by the daily Event Search run. Edit this doc to change what the run looks for.

## Interests

Professional
- NET: tech-related networking and social events. AI, software, startups and consulting. Casual formats count as much as formal ones: tech brunches, tech nights, demo nights, mixers. General small-business or sales mixers are a weak fit; list one only when nothing better is available, and say so.
- UCI: UC Irvine affiliated events, especially tech-related ones on or next to campus. Paul Merage School of Business, the Donald Bren School of Information and Computer Sciences (ICS), UCI Beall Applied Innovation at the Cove, and events at University Center.

Personal
- SOCIAL: relaxed social and hobby meetups, such as mahjong nights, matcha or coffee socials and game nights.
- WELL: wellness and workout classes. Yoga, pilates, HIIT, run clubs, outdoor bootcamps, brand-hosted wellness clubs.
- SPORT: golf and pickleball (open play, socials, clinics, mixers, small tournaments)
- NIGHT: raves and electronic music nights
- HIKE: group hikes
- BOOK: book clubs, including silent book clubs

## Filters (all must hold)

- Starts today or within the next 14 days. Skip events that start today before 10:00 AM, because the digest arrives at 8:00 AM.
- Costs $50 or less per event, before fees. Free is fine. Give the price whenever the listing or page shows it.
- Location:
  - Monday to Friday events: within about 30 miles of Irvine, CA. This covers all of Orange County (for example Irvine, Tustin, Costa Mesa, Newport Beach, Santa Ana, Orange, Anaheim, Buena Park, Fullerton, Brea, Garden Grove, Westminster, Huntington Beach, Seal Beach, Lake Forest, Mission Viejo, Laguna Beach, Dana Point, San Clemente) plus Long Beach.
  - Saturday and Sunday events: anywhere from Los Angeles County through Orange County to San Diego County.
  - The rest of Los Angeles County (anything beyond Long Beach, such as Monterey Park, West Hollywood or downtown Los Angeles) is for Saturday and Sunday only.
  - Online events: any day.
- Open to the public or to UCI students and alumni. Skip invitation-only events and events whose registration is closed. An event that is full but has a waitlist may be listed, marked "waitlist".
- Skip multi-day paid trainings and bootcamp-style courses, sales webinars, and children's events.

## Sources

How to read them: page reading in a scheduled run is refused for addresses that did not come from a web search in the same run. So search first (the site or host name plus a topic), then open the address the search returned. The addresses below tell you what to search for. Listing pages often show past events, so confirm every date.

Luma is the owner's preferred source. Most of their example events are on Luma.
