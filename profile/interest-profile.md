# Interest Profile and Filter Rules

Read by the daily Event Search run. Edit this doc to change what the run looks for.

## Interests

Professional
- NET: networking events in AI, technology and consulting
- UCI: UC Irvine events from the Paul Merage School of Business and the Donald Bren School of Information and Computer Sciences (ICS)

Personal
- SPORT: golf and pickleball (open play, socials, clinics, mixers, small tournaments)
- NIGHT: raves and electronic music nights
- FIT: workout and HIIT classes, run clubs, outdoor bootcamps
- HIKE: group hikes
- BOOK: book clubs, including silent book clubs

## Filters (all must hold)

- Starts today or within the next 14 days.
- Costs $50 or less per event, before fees. Free is fine.
- Location:
  - Monday to Friday events: within about 20 miles of Irvine, CA. This covers Irvine, Tustin, Costa Mesa, Newport Beach, Santa Ana, Orange, Lake Forest, Laguna Beach, Laguna Hills, Aliso Viejo, Mission Viejo, Laguna Niguel, Dana Point, San Juan Capistrano, Huntington Beach, Fountain Valley, Garden Grove, Anaheim and Fullerton.
  - Saturday and Sunday events: anywhere from Los Angeles County through Orange County to San Diego County.
  - Online events: any day.
- Open to the public or to UCI students and alumni. Skip invitation-only events and events whose registration is closed.
- Skip multi-day paid trainings and bootcamp-style courses, sales webinars, and children's events.
